Project Name: Customer Portal Modernization

Background:
The organization needs to modernize the customer self-service portal to reduce call center volume and improve customer visibility of requests, invoices, and support cases.

Scope:
- Replace the legacy customer portal with a responsive web portal
- Integrate customer profile, invoice, and support case data
- Deliver executive-ready weekly status reporting during implementation
- Out of scope: replacing the core ERP system

Objectives:
- Reduce support request calls by 20 percent within three months of launch
- Improve customer request visibility and response transparency
- Establish clear project controls for scope, risks, dependencies, and approvals

Deliverables:
- Approved project charter
- Integrated project plan
- Customer portal design specification
- Configured portal MVP
- UAT completion report
- Go-live readiness checklist
- RAID log
- Executive status pack

Milestones:
- Project kickoff on 2026-06-08
- Charter approval on 2026-06-15
- Design sign-off on 2026-07-03
- MVP build complete on 2026-08-14
- UAT complete on 2026-09-04
- Go-live approval on 2026-09-11

Tasks:
- Confirm project charter and success metrics | Owner: PMO Lead
- Prepare stakeholder engagement plan | Owner: Change Manager
- Capture customer portal requirements with business SMEs | Owner: Business Analyst
- Design portal information architecture | Owner: UX Lead
- Configure authentication and customer profile integration | Owner: IT Lead | requires identity provider readiness
- Build invoice and support case views | Owner: Delivery Team | depends on ERP data access
- Prepare UAT scripts and test data | Owner: QA Lead
- Validate portal MVP with pilot customers after approval from sponsor
- Prepare go-live checklist and support handover | Owner: PMO Lead

Dependencies:
- Pending ERP data access approval from Finance
- Identity provider readiness is required before integration testing
- Legal approval required for updated customer terms

Risks:
- ERP data access delay could impact MVP build and executive launch commitment
- Pilot customer availability may reduce UAT coverage
- Identity provider configuration issue could block integration testing

Assumptions:
- Business SMEs are available for weekly workshops
- Existing ERP APIs can support read-only customer data access
- PMO Lead can coordinate steering committee decisions within five business days

Constraints:
- MVP must use existing enterprise authentication
- No replacement of the ERP system is approved
- Budget approval is limited to the portal MVP phase

Stakeholders:
- Executive Sponsor: Chief Customer Officer
- Project Owner: Head of Digital
- PMO Lead: Ali PMO Prototype User
- IT Lead: Enterprise Applications Manager
- Finance Approver: Finance Operations Director
- Legal Approver: Legal Counsel

Required Approvals:
- Charter approval by Executive Sponsor
- Design sign-off by Head of Digital
- ERP data access approval by Finance
- Customer terms approval by Legal
- Go-live approval by steering committee

Acceptance Criteria:
- Portal MVP supports login, customer profile view, invoice view, and support case view
- UAT report has no open critical defects
- Go-live checklist approved by PMO Lead and Executive Sponsor
- RAID log includes owner, impact, mitigation, and escalation status for all high-impact items
